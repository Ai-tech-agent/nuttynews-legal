Privacy Policy

The Nutty News · Updated 2026-11-19

The Nutty News Publisher only connects to The Nutty News own TikTok account. It uses the basic profile information (name and avatar) to confirm which account is connected and the permission to upload and post our own videos.

We do not collect, sell or share data from any other TikTok user. Access tokens are stored privately on our own computer and are never published. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings.

Política de privacidad

Nutty News Publicador solo se conecta a la cuenta de TikTok de The Nutty News. Usa el nombre y la foto del perfil para confirmar la cuenta y el permiso para subir nuestros propios videos. No recolectamos, vendemos ni compartimos datos de nadie más. Los tokens se guardan en privado y nunca se publican.
